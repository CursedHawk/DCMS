# Editing on the page

- **Click** a part to select it. Its settings open on the right, grouped into Content, Style, Layout, Behaviour and Data. The ⓘ next to a setting says what it does.
- **Double-click** a heading, text, button, quote or badge to type into it. Click outside to finish.
- **Right-click** for everything that can be done to a part: duplicate, move up or down, wrap it in a container, unwrap it, copy and paste its style, make it reusable, delete it.
- The line above the page shows where the selected part sits. Click a name to select the part around it, or press **Esc**.
- The **+** on a selected part opens Sections, to add one below it.

**Undo** and **Redo** are at the top, and on the keyboard.
