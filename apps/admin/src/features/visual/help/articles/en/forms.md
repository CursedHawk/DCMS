# Forms

The **Form** part collects what visitors enter and sends it to the **Forms plugin**.

1. Add a Form — or a contact section, which has one.
2. In its settings, choose the **Forms plugin** and the **Form name** it sends to, the **Button label** and the **Thank-you message**.
3. Its **Fields** hold Form field parts. For each: the **Label**, the kind (text, email, a long message, a choice), whether it is **Required**, its **Help text**, and the **Field name** it is stored under.

What visitors send appears in **Forms** in the admin. Email notifications are set per form in the Forms plugin.
