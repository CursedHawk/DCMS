# Sections and parts

The palette on the left has two kinds of things to put on a page.

- **Sections** are ready-made blocks: a hero, features, prices, questions and answers, a contact form. Click one and it is added below the selected part — or at the end of the page. Everything in it is ordinary parts you can change or delete.
- **Components** are the single parts: headings, texts, pictures, buttons, layouts like Stack, Grid and Split, and data parts like Collection and Form. Drag one onto the page, or click it to add it after what is selected.

Search finds parts by what they do — try *price*, *map* or *contact*.

While you drag, a note says where the part will land, or why it cannot go there. Empty areas say what they take.
