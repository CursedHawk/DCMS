# Computer, tablet and phone

The three buttons at the top show the page as wide as a computer, a tablet or a phone.

Settings you change while **Computer** is chosen apply everywhere. Change one while **Tablet** or **Phone** is chosen, and it applies to that size and smaller only — the setting is marked as differing, and **as desktop** shows where it still follows the computer.

To undo a difference, use the reset button next to the setting.

Most layouts already adapt: a Grid or Split puts its columns under each other on a phone. Check every page on a phone before publishing.
