# Publishing

Your work is saved as you go, into a draft only you see — nothing is live until you publish.

1. **Problems** lists what would break the site: a missing page, a menu entry with nowhere to go, a picture without a description. Click a problem to go to it. Errors must be fixed first; warnings are advice.
2. **Publish** builds the site. From a draft branch, you choose what to take into the release.
3. **Deployments** shows each build, its log, and when it is live. **Redeploy** builds the release again.

**Git** in the rail shows the history of every change, and lets you work on branches.
