# Lists and detail pages

A **Collection** shows content from a plugin or a connection — events, articles, products — as a list.

1. Add a Collection from the palette (search *collection*).
2. In its settings, choose its **Content**, **How many** items to show and, if you like, **Only items tagged**.
3. Put parts into **Each item** — a heading, a text, a picture. Select one and, under *What it shows*, choose a field of the item instead of a fixed value.
4. **While loading**, **When empty** and **On error** hold what visitors see in those moments.

The canvas shows real content, so you see what you build.

**A page for each item:** add a page with an address like `/events/:slug`, and in Pages choose its **Detail page** content. Its parts bind to the item's fields the same way. A button or link in the Collection's item that goes to `/events/:slug` opens that item's page.
